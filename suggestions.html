Coping Suggestions

Personalized tools for your well-being

Most Popular

4-7-8 Breathing

Inhale 4s → Hold 7s → Exhale 8s. Activates the parasympathetic nervous system.

Box Breathing

Inhale 4s → Hold 4s → Exhale 4s → Hold 4s. Used by Navy SEALs for stress.

Deep Belly Breathing

Slow abdominal breathing to calm anxiety quickly.

5-4-3-2-1 Technique

Sensory Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

Cold Water Reset

Splash cold water on your face or wrists. The mammalian dive reflex quickly calms the nervous system.

Mindful Body Scan

Slowly bring attention from toes to head, noticing sensations without judgment.
